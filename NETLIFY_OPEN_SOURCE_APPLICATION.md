## Suggested additional details

> Healthy Food Game is a free, non-commercial, open-source educational web
> application that helps school-age children learn the principles of healthy
> eating through interactive scenarios. The project is used in Ukrainian
> public-health and educational activities and was developed for the “5 Steps
> to Healthy Eating” program. The application does not collect personal data,
> has no advertising or paid features, and is available in Ukrainian and
> English. Continued Netlify support would help keep the resource freely
> accessible to children, teachers, and participating organizations. The live
> application includes a visible “Powered by Netlify” link on every screen.
